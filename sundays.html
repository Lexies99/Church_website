Our Gathering

A Simple Gathering with an Ancient Rhythm

In an age of noise and distraction, we return to the practices and rhythms the church has engaged in for two thousand years.

1. Biblical Teaching

We believe God’s Word is alive, authoritative, and transformative. We teach verse-by-verse through whole books of the Bible, challenging disciples to align their lives with the wisdom of the Kingdom.

2. Sacramental Worship

We sing songs both ancient and modern, lift our voices together in shared confession, and remember the sacrifice and victory of Christ through weekly communion.

3. PCC Kids Discipleship

Safe, joyful environments designed specifically for infants through 5th graders to know Jesus, feel loved, and build lifelong foundations in God’s truth.

What to Expect

Your First Sunday, Step by Step

01

Arrive & Park

Look for our Passion Creek flags at Queen Creek Junior High. Friendly parking attendants will point you right to a spot.

02

Coffee & Welcome

Grab complimentary roasted coffee and tea in the courtyard. Stop by the Welcome Tent for a gift bag.

03

PCC Kids Check-in

Secure check-in process for nursery, preschool, and elementary. You'll receive matching parent/child safety tags.

04

Gathering & Worship

65-70 minutes total: worship through music, Biblical sermon, communion, prayer, and community benediction.

Find Your Church Family

The Stick Six Challenge

It takes time to know whether a church family is the right home for you. That’s why we encourage newcomers to commit to six consecutive Sundays. By the end of six weeks, you’ll know our heartbeat, experience our community, and hear God’s direction.
