Our Journey

From a Movie Theater to a Future Home

Passion Creek began with a dream to plant a gospel-centered, relational church in Queen Creek, Arizona. Our very first service took place inside a local Harkins movie theater, setting up mobile sound carts and nursery cribs at 5:30 AM each Sunday.

Over the years, God tested and refined our community through delays, relocations, and unexpected obstacles. Through each season, we learned that church isn't a building or a weekend show—it's a gathered people practicing the presence of God and learning to love their neighbors.

Today, we meet at Queen Creek Junior High while faithfully working toward our permanent campus through the Build Peace by Peace initiative, continuing to form disciples who follow Jesus in all of life.

Spiritual Formation

The Discipleship Pathway

Discipleship is not random. It is an intentional, rhythmic journey of following Jesus step by step.

STEP 01

Sunday Gathering

Gathering weekly with the whole body to sing, confess, receive communion, and hear God’s Word preached.

STEP 02

Start Here & Basics

A 4-week cohort exploring the foundations of following Jesus, biblical theology, and finding your place at PCC.

STEP 03

Together Groups

Meeting weekly in homes to share a table, practice mutual care, confess sin, and learn the nine spiritual practices.

STEP 04

Triads

Intimate groups of three people of the same gender meeting weekly for scripture memory, confession, and intense prayer.
