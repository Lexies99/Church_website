New Here?

Consider Our 3 Invitations

Taking the next step in faith or visiting a new community can feel daunting. Here are three simple, low-pressure ways to experience Passion Creek.

Invitation 01

1. Plan Your Visit

New environments can be intimidating. Watch our walkthrough video and let us know you’re coming so we can answer your questions, pre-register your children, and make your first visit comforting!

Invitation 02

2. Stick Six Challenge

Trying to find a new church home? One visit isn’t always enough. That’s why we invite you to commit to attending six Sundays in a row and see what God might do in your heart and family.

Take The Challenge
Invitation 03

3. Attend Start Here

After visiting on a Sunday, join us for a relaxed 20-minute chat with our pastors. Get to know our leadership team and hear the heartbeat and priorities of Passion Creek. Available every 1st Sunday.

Sunday Gathering

Sundays at 8:30a & 10:15a

Sundays are where we reorient ourselves back to Jesus. We gather to worship through song, sit under practical Biblical teaching, celebrate communion, and encourage one another in community.

Together Groups

Together in Homes

Faith is not meant to be walked alone. We meet weekly across Queen Creek, Gilbert, and San Tan Valley to share a warm meal together, pray, discuss Scripture, and practice the way of Jesus together.

Authentic Community Real relationships, shared meals, and prayer in local living rooms.
