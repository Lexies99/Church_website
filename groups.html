The Living Room Table

Why We Meet in Living Rooms

Sunday mornings are designed for celebration, teaching, and worship with the entire church family. But discipleship deepens around a dinner table. Together Groups are where we know and are known, confess burdens, eat together, and spur each other on toward Christlikeness.

Shared Dinner Every group begins with food and unrushed conversation.
Scripture & Practice Discussing how Sunday’s message applies to our weekly work and family.
Honest Prayer Lifting up each other’s joys, struggles, healings, and neighbors.
