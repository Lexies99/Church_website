Theology of Giving

Four Convictions Underneath The Practice

1. Everything Belongs to God

We are not owners of our wealth; we are stewards. Recognizing that God provides all things frees us from financial anxiety.

2. Gratitude Precedes Generosity

We give not out of guilt or manipulation, but out of glad overflowing praise for Christ’s generosity toward us on the cross.

3. Sacrificial & Joyful

Generosity is intended to stretch our faith, teaching us to depend more fully on the Lord rather than our bank accounts.

4. Fueling Kingdom Multiplication

Every dollar directly supports gospel preaching, discipling children and families, and planting churches.
